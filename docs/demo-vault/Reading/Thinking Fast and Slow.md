# Thinking Fast and Slow

Kahneman's tour of the two systems in our heads.

- System 1 is quick and intuitive; System 2 is slow and effortful.
- Anchoring, availability and loss aversion shape most guesses.
- Good decisions often need a checklist, not a feeling.

A practical answer is [[Atomic Habits]]: shape the environment so System 2 has less to do.
