# Weekend Sourdough

1. Friday night: mix 100 g [[Sourdough Starter]], 500 g flour, 350 g water, 10 g salt.
2. Fold every 30 minutes, four times.
3. Shape, rest in the fridge overnight.
4. Saturday: bake at 240 C in a covered pot, 20 minutes lid on, 25 off.

Tools: [[Kitchen Tools]].
