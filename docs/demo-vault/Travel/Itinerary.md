# Itinerary

1. **Mon** Arrive, walk through [[Alfama]], sunset at a viewpoint.
2. **Tue** Belem: tower, monastery, and [[Pasteis de Nata]].
3. **Wed** [[Tram 28]] early, then the cathedral and the flea market.
4. **Thu** [[Day Trip to Sintra]].
5. **Fri** Museums, long lunch, slow evening.
6. **Sat** Ferry across the river for fish on the beach.
7. **Sun** Home.
